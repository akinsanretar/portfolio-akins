About me

Driving change through method, AI and people.

A student on the Master's in Strategic Management and Change at ISM-IAE (Université Paris-Saclay), I bring my energy and my methodical skills to complex transformation projects.

My background has taken me to two complementary environments. Within the SNCF Group, at SNCF Optim’services and then OUIGO, I have helped manage conversational AI projects and their adoption by teams, in demanding, complex and constantly changing organisations. At the Île-de-France Regional Council, I worked with town halls, sports partners and elite athletes, taking part in the roll-out of Olympic and Paralympic Week as part of Paris 2024.

My watchwords: Proactivity, Responsiveness and Team spirit. I place great importance on change management: a technology solution is only valuable if frontline teams adopt it with enthusiasm.
